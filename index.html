New to MKS TCG

Start here if you’ve never touched the game before or only seen it on tables.

  • Read the About page to see what the game actually is.
  • Skim the Rulebook – focus on Setup and Turn Order.
  • At a session, ask to borrow a deck and play a guided game.

Tip: don’t worry about memorising everything. The rulebook is on your phone for quick checks mid-game.

Returning Player

You’ve played at least once and vaguely remember how Fuel, Items and Utilities work.

  • Use the Catalogue to look up any card you liked last time.
  • Revisit key sections in the Rulebook: Timing Windows and Reaction Rules.
  • Talk to whoever’s running the night about trying a different Driver/Vehicle combo.

Tip: focus on one Driver you enjoy and learn how to abuse their ability properly.

Deckbuilders & Gremlins

You’re here to optimise, break things, or brew something deeply cursed.

  • Open the Catalogue and filter by card type and rarity.
  • Check Driver and Vehicle synergies, then layer in Utilities.
  • Use the Rulebook to verify weird interactions before you start rules-lawyering in the room.

Tip: build around a plan: Fuel engine, Item loops, or pure position control. Don’t try to do everything.

Core Loop

  1. Pick a Driver and Vehicle. They define your style.
  2. Build a 40-card deck of Items, Utilities and Fuel.
  3. Each turn: draw, place Fuel, roll for movement, then use Items/Utilities.
  4. Use Fuel to power big effects, dodge attacks and manipulate positions.
  5. Reach the finish line and survive a full round without being knocked back.

The full breakdown – including edge cases and reaction rules – lives in the Rulebook.

Built for the Mario Kart Society

MKS TCG is designed to be played in a real room with real people: shouting, table politics, risky plays and last-second swings are all part of the experience.

The website exists so you can quickly look up cards, double-check a rule, or plan your next deck between sessions – not as a replacement for actually sitting down and racing.
