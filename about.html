About MKS TCG

The official trading card game designed for the Staffordshire University
Mario Kart Society. Built by fans, for fans.

What is the MKS TCG?

The MKS Trading Card Game is a custom strategy game inspired by the spirit of Mario Kart, designed to be fast, chaotic, and endlessly replayable. Every match simulates a race, with players using Drivers, Vehicles, Items, Utilities and Fuel to advance their position, disrupt opponents, and survive the madness.

The game was built specifically for the Mario Kart Society community, providing a physical collectible experience tailored to weekly sessions, tournaments, and casual play.

How the Game Works

Each player builds a 40-card deck and chooses a Driver and Vehicle. Throughout the race, players roll for movement, place Items, burn Fuel for abilities, and use Utilities to control the flow of the game.

  • Drivers provide powerful activated abilities.
  • Vehicles offer passive bonuses and define playstyle.
  • Items attack, defend, disrupt, and swing momentum.
  • Utilities shape the board, enhance strategy, and manage resources.
  • Fuel powers everything — every ability and item choice matters.

The goal is simple: reach the finish line and survive a full round without being knocked back. It's Mario Kart, but as a card game — unpredictable, tactical, and very, very spicy.

Development

The MKS TCG began as a small experimental project and grew into a full competitive game system with hundreds of cards, printing infrastructure, booster pack generation tools, and a digital catalogue.

Everything is handcrafted: card design, balancing, rarity structure, printing, packaging, gameplay rules, and this website. The goal is to deliver a polished, fully-functional TCG experience that feels official — while still being completely community-run.

The game is designed to be:

  • Easy to learn — rules that flow naturally.
  • Deep to master — deckbuilding and turn-planning matter.
  • Chaotic in the best way — true Mario Kart energy.

Vision & Future

The long-term plan is to continue expanding the card pool, introduce new sets throughout the year, and support formats such as:

  • Standard racing mode
  • Draft & sealed events
  • Seasonal expansions
  • Special society-exclusive cards

Future updates will also refine the rules, improve the printing pipeline, and expand this website with deckbuilding tools and player profiles.

The Community

The MKS TCG is made for the Mario Kart Society community — a place where players meet weekly, race, compete, laugh, and build friendships.

Without the society, this game simply wouldn’t exist. Every card, rule, balance decision, and booster pack is inspired by the people who show up every week to make this community special.
